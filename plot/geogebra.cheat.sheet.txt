Special characters for  geogebra operators that are clunky to type in manually. Copy / paste !

∧   Logical AND     (Unicode U+2227)
∨   Logical OR      (Unicode U+2228)
¬   NOT             (Unicode U+00AC)
≤   Less or equal   (U+2264)
≥   Greater or equal (U+2265)
≠   Not equal       (U+2260)
π   Pi              (U+03C0)
√   Square root     (U+221A)
∞   Infinity        (U+221E)
→   Implies         (U+2192)
↔   If and only if  (U+2194)
